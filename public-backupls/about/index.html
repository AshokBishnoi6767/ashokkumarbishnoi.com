Background

My background is computer science. That gives me the technical foundation to understand what I'm writing about — not in the sense of having a vague comfort with technology, but in the sense of being able to read documentation, follow an architecture diagram, and notice when an explanation I've been handed doesn't actually hold together.

Seven years of work across SaaS, AI, data, cybersecurity, IT and emerging technology taught me the other half: why any of it matters commercially. Technical accuracy without commercial judgement produces content that is correct and useless. Commercial instinct without technical accuracy produces content that technical buyers stop trusting by the third paragraph.

The whole job is holding both at once.

How I work

I don't believe good content is about using bigger words or publishing more articles. It's about understanding the product, the audience and the business well enough to say the right thing in the simplest possible way.

In practice that means a few things you'll notice quickly.

  • I ask uncomfortable questions early. Who is this actually for, what do they believe now, and what has to change? If those don't have clear answers, the writing won't either.
  • I'll tell you when a piece shouldn't be written. A project that produces nothing is cheaper than a project that produces something nobody uses.
  • I read the source material. Documentation, demos, sales calls, competitor pages. The research is where the quality comes from; the writing is where it becomes visible.
  • I don't promise numbers I can't influence. No guaranteed rankings, no invented conversion lifts. Every result on this site is one I can support.

Industries and formats

Most of my work is in B2B SaaS, AI, cybersecurity, data, DevTools, IT and SaaS services, FinTech, HealthTech and MarTech. I also work with marketing and content agencies that need a reliable technical or white-label writer.

Formats span the full B2B catalogue: SaaS websites and technical blogs, case studies, whitepapers, sales collateral, email campaigns, video scripts, search ads, content briefs and thought leadership.

Some of my work is ghostwritten and published under other author names, which is normal in this field and worth saying plainly.

Books and other writing

Outside client work I write fiction and poetry. Three books are in progress: 100 Drops in a Sea, a collection of a hundred haiku; Phases of the Moon and Love, short romantic verse; and Arjun Bheel: The Rise of the Darkness, a novel about a man who becomes the master of his own darkness rather than merely confronting it.

This is here because it's true and because it explains something about how I work. Writing for a business and writing for yourself teach different disciplines. One teaches you to serve a purpose. The other teaches you that a sentence can be technically correct and still not land — which turns out to matter a great deal on a homepage.

Beyond work: technology, quantum physics, philosophy, theology, psychology and logic are the things I'll talk about for far too long. Reading, cycling, swimming and hiking are what happens when someone stops me.
