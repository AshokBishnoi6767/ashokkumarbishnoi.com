Insights

Writing on B2B technology content: what works, what doesn't, and why so much of it is expensive and useless.

Everything here is written to be useful on its own. If you read a piece, take the idea, and implement it yourself without ever contacting me, that is a fine outcome and the piece did its job. Content that only makes sense as a lead magnet tends to read like one.

These are arguments rather than summaries. Some of them are things a fair number of marketers would disagree with, which is the point — a piece nobody could argue with is rarely worth reading.
