SEO / PAGE IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

Recommended internal links

From Building → Website / Digital Presence service page when those pages exist.

From Accelerating → relevant SEO, content or growth service pages.

From Advancing → Content, PPC, Social, Email and Creative pages.

From Inbound → Inbound Marketing page.

From ABM → ABM page.

From Every business has a frontier → Contact page.

From The I way → About me.

This lets the Services page become the hub while individual service pages become the deeper topical authorities.

One important change from the old version

I would not label the audience as B2B or B2C anywhere in the main journey.

That's strategically correct for what we've established.

The visitor should think:

"I understand where my business is and know what to do next."

Not:

"Am I the type of company they serve?"

The page sells capability and movement, not a demographic category.

And the central idea stays intact:

Find your frontier. Move.
