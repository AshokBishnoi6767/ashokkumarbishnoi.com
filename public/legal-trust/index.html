9. Video Opportunity

I wouldn't force a video onto this page.

This is important.

A legal/trust page shouldn't have a promotional video just because other pages do.

However, there is one video opportunity worth creating later:

"The I Standard"

Length

60–90 seconds.

Concept

A calm founder-led or voiceover film.

Not:

"Hi, we're I and we're excited to..."

Instead:

"Technically legal isn't always right."

Then move through:

Trust.

Privacy.

Fairness.

Responsibility.

Ambition.

Profit.

And finish with:

"We want to win. We just want to be able to explain how we won."

Then:

THE I STANDARD

We make brands fly.
