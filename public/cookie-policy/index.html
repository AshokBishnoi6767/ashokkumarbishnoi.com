Cookies. Explained.

We use cookies and similar technologies to make ashokkumarbishnoi.com work, understand how people use it, improve the experience and, where applicable, measure marketing activity.

This Cookie Policy explains what those technologies are, why we use them and how you can control them.

Because privacy shouldn't require a detective degree.

01 — What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can help a website remember things about your visit, recognize your browser, maintain a session, remember preferences and understand how the website is being used.

We may also use technologies that work in similar ways, including:

Pixels

Tags

Local storage

Web beacons

Similar tracking technologies

Not every technology does the same thing.

That's why we explain them by what they do, rather than hiding everything under the word "cookie."

02 — Why do we use them?

Different technologies serve different purposes.

Some are essential.

Some make the experience better.

Some help us understand what's working.

And some may help us understand whether our marketing is actually doing anything.

Here's the breakdown.

Strictly necessary

These technologies help the website function.

They may support:

Website operation

Security

Fraud prevention

Session management

Load balancing

Essential functionality

Consent preferences

Without some of these technologies, parts of the website may simply not work properly.

Necessary means necessary.

We don't use "necessary" as a convenient excuse for everything else.

Preferences

Preference technologies help the website remember choices you've made.

For example:

Language preferences

Display settings

Cookie choices

Consent preferences

Other functionality settings

The goal is simple:

You shouldn't have to make the same choice every time you visit.

Analytics

Analytics technologies help us understand how people use the website.

They may help us understand:

Which pages people visit

How visitors move through the website

Which content is useful

How long pages take to load

Where people encounter problems

Aggregate traffic patterns

How we can improve the website

Analytics should help us make better decisions.

Not spy on people.

Where consent is required, we obtain it before deploying the relevant optional technologies.

Marketing & advertising

Where implemented, marketing technologies may help us:

Measure campaigns

Understand interactions with our content

Attribute conversions

Measure advertising effectiveness

Understand which channels are generating useful activity

Deliver or measure relevant advertising

We don't believe that adding more tracking automatically creates better marketing.

More data isn't always better data.

Where applicable law requires consent for marketing or advertising technologies, we obtain the required consent before using them.

03 — Third-party technologies

Some technologies on our website may be provided by third parties.

Depending on the tools we actually use, these may include providers supporting:

Analytics

Advertising

CRM

Marketing automation

Video

Security

Website functionality

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements with us.

One important distinction

This policy describes the categories of technology we may use.

The actual technologies deployed on the website can change.

When they do, our cookie-consent mechanism and internal records should be updated accordingly.

We don't believe a policy should promise technologies that aren't actually being used.

04 — Cookie consent

Your choices matter.

Where applicable law requires consent for cookies or similar technologies, I will ask for that consent before deploying the relevant optional technologies.

Depending on your location and the technologies being used, our consent mechanism may allow you to:

Accept optional technologies

Reject optional technologies

Manage individual categories

Change your preferences later

We aim to make those choices understandable rather than burying them behind legal language.

Consent shouldn't feel like a trap.

Where consent is required, declining optional technologies should not prevent access to functionality that does not reasonably depend on them.

05 — What happens when you reject cookies?

Some cookies are necessary for the website to operate.

Others aren't.

If you reject optional cookies or similar technologies, some features may behave differently.

For example, certain preferences may not be remembered, analytics may not function, or embedded third-party functionality may behave differently.

That's okay.

A privacy choice should still be a choice.

06 — Managing your preferences

If our website provides a cookie preference centre, you can use it to review or change your choices.

You can also manage cookies through your browser.

Most modern browsers allow you to:

View stored cookies

Delete cookies

Block cookies

Allow cookies from specific websites

Receive notifications before cookies are stored

Browser controls vary.

And blocking everything isn't always the best experience.

The choice is yours.

07 — Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a Do Not Track signal.

There is not one universally accepted technical standard governing how every website must respond to these signals.

As a result, our response may depend on:

The technology being used

The browser

Applicable legal requirements

The particular privacy signal

Where applicable law requires us to recognize a particular privacy preference signal, we will take reasonable steps to do so.

08 — Privacy and cookies

Cookies can sometimes be connected with information that relates to an identifiable individual.

That's why cookies aren't just a technical issue.

They're a privacy issue.

Our broader privacy practices are explained in our Privacy Policy.

Our approach is straightforward:

Collect less.

Protect more.

Explain what we're doing.

Respect people's choices.

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada identifies consent, limiting collection, limiting use and disclosure, safeguards and openness among the core principles governing personal-information practices under PIPEDA.

The OPC also specifically recommends notifying website users about cookies and their purposes and, as a best practice, seeking express opt-in consent for cookies or similar technologies where appropriate.

09 — International visitors

I is based in Canada, but the internet doesn't stop at the border.

If you access our website from another country or jurisdiction, additional privacy or cookie requirements may apply.

Depending on the circumstances, this may affect:

Which technologies we use

How consent is collected

Which choices are presented

How information is processed

Which privacy rights apply

Where required, we will implement appropriate mechanisms for the jurisdictions in which we operate.

Different places. Different rules.

We respect them.

10 — How long do cookies stay?

Cookies can have different lifespans.

Some disappear when you close your browser.

Others remain for a defined period.

The retention period depends on what the technology does and why it is being used.

We aim to keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its identified purpose and applicable legal requirements.

Our privacy practices are designed around limiting collection, use, disclosure and retention where applicable.

11 — Things change

Websites change.

Technology changes.

Our tools change.

Privacy requirements change.

So this policy may change too.

When we make changes, we'll update the Last Updated date at the top of this page.

If a change materially affects how we use cookies or similar technologies, we will take additional steps where appropriate or required.

A privacy policy shouldn't be fossilized.

Neither should the technology behind it.
