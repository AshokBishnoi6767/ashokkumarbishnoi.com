Start a project

Send me the product, the audience and the problem. I'll tell you where I can help.

Six questions. None of them require you to have decided what you want yet — "we're not sure, here's what's going wrong" is a perfectly good brief and often a more honest one.

I read every message personally and reply within two working days. If I'm not the right person for what you need, I'll say so directly rather than stretching the scope to fit.

What happens next

  1. You send the six answers.
  2. I reply within two working days with my read on the problem.
  3. If it looks like a fit, we book a call.
  4. You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

No automated sequence, no CRM, no follow-up drip. One person reading one email.

Before you write

Things worth mentioning

  • What you've already tried and what happened.
  • Whether anyone internally owns content today.
  • Who has to approve published work.
  • Anything you've been told by a previous agency that you didn't quite believe.

Things you don't need

  • A finished brief.
  • A budget figure, though it saves a round-trip.
  • A decision about which service you want.
  • Any content of your own to show me. If it doesn't exist yet, that's the situation, not a problem.
